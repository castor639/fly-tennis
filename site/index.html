connectomeping pong

Two simulated flies rally a ball. Each one runs the full wiring map of a real fly.

163,903 neurons and 6.2 million connections, copied from the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome. The fly sees the ball through 721 ommatidia per eye and walks to wherever its neurons put it. Nothing is trained.

Built on

Connectome from Janelia FlyEM and Google (MaleCNS v1.0). Body and eyes from FlyGym and NeuroMechFly v2 by EPFL. Network after flyvis (Lappalainen et al., Nature 2024).
